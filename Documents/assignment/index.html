Privacy & Data Protection Notice

At Shamba Direct, we are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Data Protection Act (2019) and guidelines from the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Data We Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number) for account registration
  • Location data for delivery purposes
  • Cookies for session management and user experience improvement

How We Use Your Data

  • To process orders and facilitate transactions between buyers and sellers
  • To communicate about orders, deliveries, and platform updates
  • To improve our services and user experience

Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act (2019), you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction, in compliance with the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act (2018).
